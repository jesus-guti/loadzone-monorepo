# Product mark is an abstract load symbol, not a club badge

Staff sign-in and the staff shell need a LoadZone identity that is not a letter cut out of a shield and not a football illustration. We will use a **Product mark**: an abstract load or zone symbol plus the wordmark “LoadZone”, in the existing sage brand family. Interface type stays Geist.

The mark appears on staff sign-in, on the other signed-out staff screens (forgot password, reset password, invite), in the authenticated shell where the product name is shown today, and as the favicon. It does not replace **Club** or **Team** logos.

Rejected: a football symbol (ball, pitch, or shirt), a wordmark with no symbol, and a new typeface that replaces Geist across the staff product.
