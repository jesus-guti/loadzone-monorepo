Type: grilling  
Status: resolved  
Blocked by: 03, 04

## Question

Should the Streak Cromo adopt a TCG-like **frame** (borders that read as a physical card) and **selective foil** (metallic chrome that catches light on tilt, not a wash over the whole portrait), and which identity pieces are foil vs ink?

Yes: rectangular outer frame, arched portrait window inside (hybrid). Foil on tilt on **frame, LOADZONE pill, rank seal, dorsal overprint**. Portrait stays matte. Player name and Team name stay ink. Not Pokémon art or copied clip-paths. Glossary updated on **Streak Cromo**. Prototype is blocked on this anatomy.
